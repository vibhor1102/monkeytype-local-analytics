accounting & privacy

Counts belong to expected characters, including spaces and punctuation—not physical keys or fingers. Early word endings count at the current position; untyped letters are not invented as attempts. Extra insertions and manual deletion actions are separate.

Practice focus compares error rates only after 20 attempts per character. Smaller samples remain visible in the table. These are practice hints; this rate can differ from Monkeytype’s overall accuracy.

Statistics belong to this browser profile across Monkeytype sign-ins. No raw text, account identifiers, uploads, or sync. Displayed aggregates are visible to the page. Uninstalling or clearing browser data can erase storage; keep a backup.

collection status & compatibility

Finished standard tests are supported. Zen, bailed-out tests, Funbox, opposite-shift, composition/IME, and surrogate-pair characters are excluded. Automatic input/deletion is excluded. Only tests started after connection are eligible. “Default” layout does not identify your operating system layout.

Pending saves live in the typing tab; keep it open until saved. Polling can miss tests completed entirely between checks. The latest 10,000 test hashes prevent duplicates. After an extension update, reload idle Monkeytype tabs to reconnect.

backups & data
