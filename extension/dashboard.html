How to read your statistics

Each character insertion is an attempt at the character Monkeytype expected at that position. A correction keeps the original mistake; typing the replacement adds another attempt. Spaces and punctuation count. Early word endings count at the current position; untyped remaining letters are not counted as attempts.

Extra keystrokes beyond the target word are listed separately. Error rates here describe character attempts and can differ from Monkeytype’s overall accuracy. For a useful rate comparison, raise “Min. attempts” to 20 or more.

Finished standard tests are supported. Zen, bailed-out tests, Funbox, opposite-shift and composition input are excluded. This tracks characters, not inferred physical finger movements. “Layout setting” is Monkeytype’s configured layout; “default” does not identify your operating system’s actual layout.

Data belongs to this browser profile, across Monkeytype sign-ins. Raw test text, passwords and account identifiers are not stored. No analytics are uploaded or synced. Browser data clearing or uninstalling can erase them; export a backup to keep them.
